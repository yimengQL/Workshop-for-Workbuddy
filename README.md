Before you start to use Workbuddy, there are something you must know!
1. All materials you upload to Workbuddy may be used to train their LLM models or save in their service.   
   Never upload your private data or material to Workbuddy such as Trade secrets, Your confidential personal information and so on.

2. Exercise caution when granting Workbuddy access permissions.   
   AI agents may sometimes misunderstand your instructions, messing up your computor.   
   Before you start using it to complete a task, it's important to let it demonstrate its thought process and what it's going to do.   
   Then, according to its plan, you grant it the authority to proceed to the next step.

4. Data backup.   
   If you grant AI permission to modify your computer's documents, please back up your data before starting the task to prevent the AI's results from being unexpected or causing unforeseen problems, thus avoiding irreversible file damage.

5. Always designate a workplace for AI agents.   
   It can, to some extent, prevent AI agents from reading sensitive data on your computer.


Here are some simple tasks to help you experience how Workbuddy works.

1. analysis

import the Expert “Career Coach”.

prompt： Analyze my qualifications, academic background, skills, work experience, projects, and achievements against all currently available internship   opportunities in Hong Kong. For each internship position, evaluate my suitability by comparing my profile with the job requirements and responsibilities.   Generate a comprehensive report that includes: Company name and internship title, Job description and key responsibilities, Required and preferred qualifications, Match score (0-100%) based on my profile, Strengths that align with the role, Skill gaps or missing qualifications, Recommendations to improve my competitiveness, Application priority (High / Medium / Low). Finally, rank all internship opportunities by overall suitability and provide a summary of the top opportunities that best match my background and career goals.

2. create CV

import the skill "canvas design"

prompt: Based on the job title (XX), please create a tailored CV for me. Highlight the most relevant skills, experience, and achievements that align with this role, and tailor the wording of the summary, work experience, and key qualifications to match the position's requirements and keywords. Use a clear, professional structure that is easy to scan, and ensure the content stays truthful to my background while emphasizing strengths most relevant to the target job. Use my resume as the primary source of truth and provide evidence-based evaluations rather than generic assessments.

3. assistant

Prompt: Please help me extract the CV files from the workshop files folder on my desktop. Before extracting, please provide the path to the file you intend to extract from.
